Word Categories

Measure words, frequency, and quantity

Measure Words

Chinese Pinyin Meaning Example
次 cì Number of occurrences 去过三次 = been there 3 times
遍 biàn Times through, start to finish 看三遍 = watch/read it 3 times through
回 huí Occasions (conversational) 我去过两回 = I've been twice
下 xià A brief action / one go 看一下 = take a look
个 (個) gè General counter (things, people) 三个人 = 3 people
件 jiàn Items, matters, clothing 三件衣服 = 3 pieces of clothing
本 běn Bound items (books, magazines) 两本书 = 2 books
杯 bēi Cups / glasses of 一杯咖啡 = a cup of coffee
瓶 píng Bottles of 一瓶水 = a bottle of water
张 (張) zhāng Flat objects (paper, tickets, tables) 一张票 = a ticket
条 (條) tiáo Long, thin objects (roads, fish, pants) 一条路 = a road
只 (隻) zhī Animals, one of a pair 一只猫 = a cat
位 wèi People (polite / formal) 三位客人 = 3 guests
块 (塊) kuài Chunks, pieces; yuan (money) 十块钱 = 10 yuan

Frequency

Chinese Pinyin Meaning Example
总是 (總是) zǒngshì Always 他总是迟到 = He's always late
通常 tōngcháng Usually 我通常八点起床 = I usually get up at 8
经常 (經常) jīngcháng Often 她经常看书 = She often reads
有时候 (有時候) yǒu shíhou Sometimes 有时候我走路去 = Sometimes I walk there
很少 hěn shǎo Rarely 我很少喝酒 = I rarely drink
从不 (從不) cóng bù Never 我从不吃辣 = I never eat spicy food

Quantity

Chinese Pinyin Meaning Example
所有 suǒyǒu All 所有人都来了 = Everyone came
很多 hěn duō Many / A lot 很多人喜欢 = Many people like it
一些 yìxiē Some 我买了一些水果 = I bought some fruit
几个 (幾個) jǐ gè A few 我有几个朋友 = I have a few friends
少 shǎo Few / Little 人很少 = There are few people
没有 (沒有) méiyǒu None / Don't have 我没有时间 = I have no time

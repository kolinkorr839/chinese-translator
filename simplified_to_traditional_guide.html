Simplified → Traditional Mental Conversion Guide

A systematic pattern-based approach for Traditional Chinese readers to effortlessly read Simplified text without rote memorization.

1. High-Frequency One-to-One Character Changes
A small core group of Simplified forms accounts for a massive percentage of everyday reading. Master these first:

2. Component System Recognition (The Key Rule)
Simplification usually alters entire components. Learning component rules instantly unlocks hundreds of characters:
讠 → 言 (Speech Radical)
说 → 說  |  话 → 話  |  语 → 語
读 → 讀  |  讲 → 講  |  词 → 詞
饣 → 食 (Food/Eat Radical)
饭 → 飯  |  饮 → 飲  |  馆 → 館
饿 → 餓  |  饱 → 飽  |  饼 → 餅
钅 → 金 (Metal Radical)
钱 → 錢  |  银 → 銀  |  铁 → 鐵
钟 → 鐘  |  错 → 錯  |  镜 → 鏡
纟 → 糸 (Silk/Thread Radical)
红 → 紅  |  纸 → 紙  |  级 → 級
细 → 細  |  练 → 練  |  经 → 經
3. Universal / Unchanged Characters & Dual Mode Reading
Many common characters remain 100% identical. Train your brain to only trigger conversion on simplified components:

Unchanged Forms: 人、我、你、他、日、月、山、天、中、大、小、好、明、去、今、校

Simplified 我今天去学校。
Mental Map 我今天去學校。 (Only 学 triggers mental conversion!)
4. Watch Out: "Dangerous" One-to-Many Merges
Some Simplified characters represent multiple Traditional characters depending on context. Learn these as word units:
后 → 後 (After/Behind) vs 后 (Queen)
后来 → 後來  |  皇后 → 皇后
发 → 發 (Emit/Develop) vs 髮 (Hair)
发展 → 發展  |  头发 → 頭髮  |  发现 → 發現
台 → 臺 (Platform/Taiwan) vs 台 (Stage/Desk) vs 颱 (Typhoon)
台湾 → 臺灣  |  舞台 → 舞臺  |  台风 → 颱風
5. Practice Exercise & High-Frequency Compound Recognition
Test your mental conversion speed without looking at the traditional text first:
Practice 我们已经学了很多东西，但是还需要继续练习。
Target 我們已經學了很多東西，但是還需要繼續練習。
